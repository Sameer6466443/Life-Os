Beyond the textbooks

The Patrick Jane approach.
Observe. Question. Deduce.

Patrick Jane, from The Mentalist, is fascinating because he pays attention to details others overlook, notices inconsistencies and tests his assumptions before reaching a conclusion.

The Mentalist Lab brings that spirit into everyday practice: sharpen observation, train memory, recognise patterns and reason from evidence. The goal is not to read minds — it is to think more carefully.

Inspired by a fictional character. Real skills develop through practice, feedback and critical thinking.

01 / ObserveNotice details and changes.
02 / RememberPractise recall and attention.
03 / Find patternsConnect clues and sequences.
04 / DeduceSeparate evidence from guesses.
05 / QuestionTest assumptions fairly.
06 / ReasonCompare possible explanations.
